All the questions

    How it goes

    1. You read one question. The lines it is about are right there, next to why we noticed it and why it may be fine.
    2. You decide. Ask about it, say you are not sure, or set it aside. You can undo any choice.
    3. The letter builds itself. Each question you keep is added, in plain words, to a calm letter you can edit.

    What it will not do

    • Call a charge wrong because it costs a lot.
    • Add up amounts in question into a total or promise savings.
    • Accuse anyone. The letter asks questions.
